# Slate — Demo Call Script (v2, Value Equation)

Value = (Dream Outcome × Likelihood) ÷ (Time × Effort).
The prospect is silently asking: **What will I make? How do I know it'll work? How long? What's expected of me?**
Every question below collects their words for one of those slots. Pitch by playing their words back.

**Rule: demo only what they named. Every unasked feature shown = added perceived effort.**

---

## OPEN (5 min) — let them monologue the pain

- "Walk me through what happens after a session ends — from 'goodbye' to money in your account."
- "What are you using today to run it all?" *(capture: Calendly / spreadsheet / Venmo / paper / nothing)*
- "How many students right now, and what do you tutor?" *(capture — feeds our stats)*

> ✍️ Write down their exact pain phrase. It's the headline of your close.

## DREAM OUTCOME (5 min) — "the after-state, in their words"

- "If billing, scheduling, and chasing payments took zero minutes a week — what would you do with that time?"
- "Where do you want this to be in a year — more students, higher rates, or same size with less headache?"
- (If they mention money:) "What would one extra student a week be worth to you?"

> ✍️ Capture: revenue dream or lifestyle dream? Pitch to the one they said.

## LIKELIHOOD (5 min) — surface doubts BEFORE the demo

- "Have you tried anything before to fix this — an app, a template, a VA? What happened?"
- "What feels riskiest about moving students + billing into one system?"
- "How do parents pay you today — and how would they react to a card link?"

> ✍️ Capture the #1 objection. The demo must SHOW that exact thing working. Proof > claims.
> Risk collapse line: "Early access is free, no card — worst case, you're back on your spreadsheet in ten minutes."

## TIME (3 min) — what "fast" means to them

- "When's your next billing crunch — end of this month?"
- "If you started today, what's the FIRST thing you'd want to just stop doing?"

> ✍️ Their first-win definition. Close with: "Set your rate today → your next billing day already doesn't happen."

## EFFORT (2 min) — find the ceiling

- "How much setup would kill it for you — an hour? An afternoon?"
- "What do you use now that you'd hate to give up?" *(Calendly link → 'Slate's booking link is the same motion')*
- "Who else would have to change anything — parents, students, a co-tutor?"

> ✍️ Quote UNDER their ceiling: "Rates + students in ~20 min. Parents just get a link — nothing to install."

## DEMO (10 min) — their movie, not a feature tour

Show, in order:
1. The thing that answers their **pain phrase**
2. The thing that kills their **#1 objection**
3. The **first win** they named
Stop there.

## CLOSE (5 min) — the equation, in their words

> "You said **[dream]**. You were worried about **[objection]** — you just watched it work.
> You'd skip **[next billing day / the chasing texts]**. Setup is **[under their ceiling]**, free, no card.
> Want me to set you up right now while we're on the call?"

*(Setting them up live on the call = time-to-value of zero. Always offer it.)*
